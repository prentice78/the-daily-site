The Daily

The Daily is a private dashboard that one person runs on their own home network. It is not a public service, and no one else can sign in to it.

Privacy policy

The app asks Google for read-only access to its owner's calendar, Gmail and YouTube account.

  • Calendar: today's and tomorrow's events are shown on the dashboard.
  • Gmail: when the owner runs a scan, the app reads the sender, date, subject and preview line of ticket and event messages found by narrow searches.
  • YouTube: subscriptions and liked videos are read during a scan.

Data is stored only on the owner's home computer. During a scan, filtered text with contact details removed is sent to the Anthropic API to identify favorite performers and hobbies. Google data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else.

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account security page.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms
